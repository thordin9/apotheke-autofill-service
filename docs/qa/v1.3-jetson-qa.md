# v1.3 Jetson QA: autofill service on xavier12

- **Service:** v1.3, image `apotheke-autofill:jetson-5fabeaa` (main `5fabeaa`, Merge #20; `app/` and `openapi.yaml` identical to the reviewed head `fc94ea7`). Rollback image: `jetson-517352f`.
- **Board:** xavier12 (Jetson Xavier, Ollama 0.15.6, `qwen2.5vl-jetson:3b`). Container `autofill`, `BACKEND=ollama`, `ALLOWED_MODELS=qwen2.5vl-jetson:3b`, `IMAGE_SIZES=448,560*,1024`, `BACKEND_TIMEOUT_S=45`, `OLLAMA_KEEP_ALIVE=-1`, `OLLAMA_LOG_RAW=0`.
- **Window:** 2026-10-19, 21:37–21:59 EEST. The board was rebooted at 21:31:52 before the deploy; the container started at 21:35:49.
- **Client side:** requests go from the QA box over the network through the board's 30 Mbit/s inbound cap. The bearer token is read from a file at runtime; it isn't printed or stored anywhere. Apotheke v2.4.1 runs from a separate clone with a fresh, empty DATA_DIR, never from the production install.
- **Prior QA:** stub/CI QA in `docs/qa/v1.3-stub-qa.md` (round 2 PASS on #20 / `fc94ea7`).

**Verdict: PASS.** Every planned check passed on hardware. Two low-severity input-hygiene findings and some model-quality notes are listed below; none of them blocks v1.3.

### 2.3 Identity fields on readable-brand photos (model quality, not service behaviour)

- **Brand:** brand correct for 9/9 photos with a visible brand at 560: sony, tefal, casio, panadol, Raspberry Pi, ibm, reebok, ryobi (the sidecar says not readable), plus "" for the hammer and lamp.
- **Model:** model correct for Sony `cdp-ce525` and Casio `fx-991EX` at 560. At 1024 the Casio was misread as `fx-891ex`, and at 448 as `fx-s915ex`.
- **Serial:**
  - Sony `5104890` correct at every size.
  - Casio: the series name `CLASS5WIZ` (and once a display value `0.5461446896`) came back as a "serial".
  - Raspberry Pi: the serial differs every run (`GP10`, `2468G-RP140`, `2018`, `20953-RPI4B`; ground truth `2ABCB-RPI4B`, readable only at 1024). "never guess" is not fully honoured.
- **Name and category:** the name contained a brand once ("Raspberry Pi 4 Model B" at 1024). Panadol was categorized as Documents / Küche in some lists.

## 3. Findings

| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |

| F-1 | Low (pre-existing on main) | A lone surrogate (JSON `\ud83d`) in `context.categories` or `context.locale` → HTTP 500 with a traceback in `docker logs` (UnicodeEncodeError in httpx `encode_json`). Ollama is not called and the service keeps working; the traceback has no context or answer text. | In `parse_context`, reject strings that don't encode as UTF-8 with 400 `bad_request` (or drop the item). |

The client sent them; Apotheke matches the category by name, so there's no impact today. | Apply the identity-field cleaning (strip Cc/Cs/bidi, trim) to `categories`/`tags` on input. |
| F-3 | Info | Model quality of serial/model on small labels (2.3). A service-side fix isn't possible. | For Apotheke v2.5, show `serial_number`/`model_number` as editable suggestions and never auto-save them; prefer 1024 for label shots. |
| F-4 | Info | Ollama logs "flash attention enabled but not supported by gpu" on every load. | Harmless; leave the config as is. |
| F-5 | Info | Two containers on the board aren't part of the service: `recursing_maxwell` (Exited, 46 h) and `temp-entry` (`ghcr.io/openclaw/openclaw:latest`, Created). | DevOps to confirm they are expected. |
